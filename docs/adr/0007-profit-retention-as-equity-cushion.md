# 0007. Profit Retention as Dynamic Equity Cushion (Supersedes ADR 0005 Sweeping)

We retain all realized profits within the trading account rather than systematically sweeping them out, while keeping single-symbol Tier 1-3 sizing strictly fixed at 10U, 20U, and 60U. This organic growth of equity automatically widens the account margin buffer (lowering effective leverage and portfolio margin ratio during deep market crashes) without exposing larger nominal risk at cycle peaks.
